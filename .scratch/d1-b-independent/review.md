## Criterion used

The gate's B is "该观察的字段（key/content/ref/meta）确实支持该行的语义断言".
In this database the fields carry **no** semantics: `observations.content` is NULL
on 42,396 / 42,396 rows, `meta` is `{}`, `key` is a phash. The only carrier of the
semantic payload is `ref`, a JPEG path. So B was judged **by looking at the
referenced frame**, per row, at 1000 px full-frame plus targeted full-resolution
crops for text and clothing.

- **pass** — every checkable assertion the row makes about its cited frame(s) is
  borne out: scene, subjects, action, garments/colours, overlays (watermark `ever`,
  centre `×`, letterbox bands, haze), quoted on-screen text and numbers. Where the
  row also claims continuity or a cut, the claimed previous state had to exist in
  the preceding small row(s) at scene level.
- **fail** — a cited frame contradicts an assertion, or the row asserts something
  about its own observation set that the set does not contain.
- **unjudgeable** — frame missing/undecodable or content too vague to check. **None.**

The `window` column is the gate's mechanical half (A) on the same rows: in-window
references / all references. I did not re-derive A's 200-row sample; these numbers
are indicative only and are not a D1 A verdict.

## Sensitivity — how this verdict could invert

Two readings, each defensible, would make D1 B fail:

1. **Citation-scope reading.** `docs/SCHEMA.md:89` says "content 与 intent 里出现的
   任何可核对断言，都要能在 obs_ids 指向的原始观测里找到依据". 42 of 50 rows carry a
   comparative or continuity clause (`从X切至Y`, `相较上一帧`, `仍为`) whose base frame
   is **not** in that row's `obs_ids` — it is the previous message's observation.
   Under the literal reading those 42 rows fail, and so does B. I judged the "current
   frame" side (which is what `obs_ids` cites) and used the preceding rows only to
   check that the claimed previous state exists; that is the reading the gate's
   wording ("该观察…支持该行的语义断言") best supports, but it is a reading.
2. **Identity-epithet reading.** 17 of 50 rows use the standing label `浅色外套男子`
   for the arcade dancer. On four of them the cited frame shows a short-sleeve white
   T-shirt and jeans with no coat at all (frame for obs 14917 = row 12; obs 30783 =
   row 36; and the previous-frame side of rows 14 and 43). I counted these as a
   label artifact on an unambiguous referent and did **not** fail them; counted
   as assertions, B drops to 44 / 50 = 88 %.

Under reading 1 or 2, D1 B is **not met**. Under the reading stated in §Criterion,
it is met at 96 %. The root owns which reading the milestone adopts; a pass recorded
from this review should record which one it used.

## Findings that are not verdicts

1. **B is not decidable from the frozen snapshot alone.** B's evidence is the JPEG at
   `observations.ref`; those files live in `~/backseat-data/frames/` (6.0 GB) and are
   **not** hashed by `MANIFEST.json`, which pins only `backseat.db`. A row can pass
   today and its evidence rot tomorrow without the snapshot hash noticing. For an
   independent review this is the load-bearing defect: the claim "50 rows independently
   judged" is bound to a revision only for the database, not for the pixels.
2. **The observation rows hold no semantic field B could use.** `content` NULL,
   `meta {}`, `key` = phash on every row. The SCHEMA's field list for B
   (`key/content/ref/meta`) therefore reduces to `ref`, i.e. an image outside the
   snapshot. Anyone re-running this review needs the 6 GB of frames, not the 25 MB db.
3. **2 / 60 references fall outside their own message window**, both in msg 620
   (+12.84 s past `ts_end`). Consistent with `witness.process_batch` taking
   `ts_start=rows[0]`, `ts_end=rows[-1]` over an id-ordered fetch while `ts_wall` is
   not monotone in `id`; the sample found no inverted window, so the 12 historic
   inverted windows reported elsewhere are rare enough to miss at n = 50.
4. **Continuity words are anchored to the last same-scene frame, not to the previous
   frame.** Clear cases in the sample: row 45 (`仍为高窗大厅`, previous frame = bar
   interior, obs 36915), row 50 (`仍是蓝砖拱廊`, previous frame = man at the
   chain-link fence, obs 41672), row 47's `from` and row 23's `from` (a frame two rows
   back). Harmless for a narrative, but it means "still" and "cut from" in `content`
   cannot be read as claims about the immediately preceding message.
5. **Row 1 shows the reverse risk**: its numbers (`38 links`, `37 indexed`) look
   fabricated until the terminal text is read at 2× — the frame does say "a total of
   38 … resulting in 37 indexed". Judging at frame resolution alone would have
   produced a false fail. Any reviewer running at thumbnail resolution will
   misjudge text-bearing terminal rows in both directions.
